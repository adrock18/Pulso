# Proposal

## Why

The current frontend feels like a flat product listing. It lacks the browsing experience of a real consumer platform — no gender segmentation, no visual hierarchy for discovery, no sense of journey from landing to product to review. Users need clear paths based on who they are (men/women) and what they want to do (browse, compare, review).

## What Changes

- **Gender-segmented navigation**: Men's Grooming / Women's Care as primary entry points, not just flat category tabs
- **Homepage redesign**: Discovery-first layout with hero, featured collections, trending reviews, and quick-compare CTA — not a marketing landing page
- **Catalog overhaul**: Sidebar filters, grid/list toggle, better product cards with key specs visible, breadcrumb navigation
- **Improved page layouts**: Consistent spacing, better visual rhythm, proper section hierarchy across all pages
- **Mobile-responsive refinements**: Navigation, catalog grid, and comparison layout work cleanly on smaller screens

## Out of Scope

- Backend changes (all static data)
- New features (no new functionality, just better presentation of existing pages)
- Image sourcing (placeholder images remain)
